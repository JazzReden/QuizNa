# Long-bond write-in answer forms

Teachers can select **Long bond · 8.5 × 13 in** or **A4 · separate essay pages** under New sheet size. Long bond is the default for newly prepared sheets in the interface. Existing sheets retain their original size, identity, option order, and scan coordinates; choosing a new size does not rewrite a previously printed QR.

The 60-question example (40 multiple-choice, 15 true/false, five essays) fits one long-bond answer sheet: two objective columns and five short written-response boxes. Names and sections are handwritten on blank lines. The QR still identifies the assigned registered student; a small assignment reference helps teachers distribute the correct sheet. This is not handwriting recognition or anonymous enrollment. Question prompts remain on the separate question paper. Essay responses require teacher transcription/review and manual grading.

Writing space is suited to short paragraphs. Longer responses, too many items, wider option sets, or dense alternating headers use additional pages rather than shrinking marks. Essay-only exams use full-width writing pages. Teachers can choose A4 for larger essay areas.

Layout revision 3 stores long-bond page geometry (1000×1529) alongside its rows. The renderer and optical reader derive bottom marker positions from the frozen page height. Revisions 1 and 2 keep their previous geometry. The browser uses a named 8.5×13-inch print page; the provided sample PDF has an exact 612×936-point media box. Print at Actual size / 100%, not Legal (8.5×14).
